# Nodevora Customer Rewards for WooCommerce 4.8.6

Turn repeat purchases into lasting customer rewards. Nodevora gives your WooCommerce store a focused loyalty program: customers earn points on eligible purchases, redeem them for discounts, and follow their rewards balance and points history in My Account.

## Built for your WooCommerce store

- **Earn and redeem loyalty points:** configure earning rates, redemption rules, minimum points and maximum discounts.
- **Customer rewards and history:** give customers a clear view of their balance, reward value and recent activity.
- **Classic and Blocks support:** offer loyalty rewards in both classic WooCommerce cart/checkout and modern Cart and Checkout Blocks, with HPOS compatibility.
- **Advanced Mode and developer options:** manage detailed rules, exclusions and access settings, with diagnostics and shortcode controls for store customization.
- **Design customization:** choose presets, colors, button and hover styles, spacing and component-specific appearance settings.
- **Bonuses and expiration:** reward registration, first orders and approved product reviews, and configure how long points remain available.
- **Points adjustments:** add or remove customer points from the administration panel.
- **Customer notifications:** keep customers informed about loyalty activity by email.
- **Flexible placement:** use the six Nodevora shortcodes to display balances, reward values, history, account summaries, loyalty cards and notices.
- **Backup management:** export loyalty data, preview an archive and confirm its import through the administration interface.
